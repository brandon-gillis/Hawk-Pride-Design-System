Website · Trail page proposal · 6 Oct 2026

Uphill Both Ways

Research on the Jeep Badge of Honor program and the trail, an audit of the current prototype page, and a proposed page: sections, copy, graphics and the facts we still need from the owner.

01 · What we learned

Research findings

How Badge of Honor works

  • Free Jeep program. Owners download the app, register the Jeep by VIN, check in at the trail, complete it, then request the badge in the app.
  • Since 2025 there are three tiers. Core trails are permanent and earn a physical metal badge. Limited trails run one year with a limited badge. Digital trails earn app points only.
  • Look-alike badges are sold online. The only official badge comes through the app, so people want a clear how-to.

Uphill Both Ways on the record

  • Added to the program in spring 2025, Alabama's newest BOH trail alongside Stony Lonesome #404/#421 and Morris Mountain.
  • Rated 6 of 10 on Jeep's scale. Guides list 1–2 hours.
  • Terrain reported: tight switchbacks, off-camber climbs, slick rock ledges, mud. Steep and rock-focused.
  • Name plays on the old "walked to school uphill both ways" line. It fits the brand's playful-but-serious voice.

How people experience it

  • No information. Road Rash Off Road searched for guides, videos and tips before their run and found nothing.
  • Weather changes everything. After an overnight storm the 6/10 felt much harder. Their group ran it in reverse to keep momentum.
  • Winching and breakage. Winch lines on most obstacles; a front diff and a bumper mount lost.
  • Crew matters. Spotters and teamwork were the story they told.
  • "Worth it for a badge?" One creator's video asks this directly. The page should answer it.

What this means for the page

  • Riders arrive with no reliable source. Hawk Pride should own the facts: trailhead, direction, conditions, rig minimums, check-in.
  • Conditions are the deciding variable. A visible trail status (dry / wet / closed) is the most useful single feature.
  • The badge is the hook; the weekend is the sale. Turn badge chasers into admission + camping bookings.

Sources: Trails Offroad BOH list · North Georgia Jeepin' · Road Rash Off Road trail report · GladiatorUp Southeast guide · JL Wrangler Forums · YouTube runs by Road Rash, Switchback Offroad, Silver Dollar Adventures

3

Earn the badge

Numbered four-step row with the Jeep partner mark (as supplied, on white). The biggest gap on the web today.

How to earn it
1. Get the Jeep Badge of Honor app and add your Jeep by VIN.
2. Buy park admission and sign your waiver.
3. Check in on the app at the UBW trailhead.
4. Finish the trail, then request your badge in the app.
Good to know: Official badges only come through the app. Not driving a Jeep? You can still run the trail; you just can't earn the badge.
Graphics
Step numbers in display type; Lucide smartphone, ticket, map-pin, badge-check.
Data
Need Is there cell signal at the trailhead for check-in? If not, say so.
4

Dry vs wet

Two side-by-side columns. Answers the first-hand reports that rain turns a 6 into something harder.

Dry
Ledges grip. Most lines go with lockers and good tire placement. Expect a winch pull or two.
Wet
Rock gets slick and climbs turn to clay. Plan to winch most obstacles and double your time. If it's raining hard, ride something else today.

Need owner to confirm wording; whether UBW closes after heavy rain.

6

Rig and crew

Two checklists: minimum vs recommended. Clear minimums protect the park and the rider.

Rig
  • Tire size Need
  • Front and rear lockers recommended
  • Winch with rated recovery points
  • Rock sliders / skid plates
Crew + gear
  • At least two vehicles
  • A spotter on foot for ledges
  • Radios (GMRS / CB)
  • Tree saver, snatch block, soft shackles, spare parts
  • Air down; bring a way to air up

Icon gaps already logged (roadmap item 9): winch point, rock crawl, air-down. This section is where they'd first appear.

05 · For the owner

Open questions

  • Is UBW a Core trail (permanent, metal badge) or Limited (one year)? If Limited, when does it end?
  • Trail length, start and end points, GPS track, and names of the main obstacles.
  • Intended direction. Is reverse allowed?
  • Minimum tire size and whether a winch is required or recommended.
  • Does UBW close after heavy rain? Who updates trail status, and how often?
  • Cell signal at the trailhead for app check-in.
  • Any guided runs or event-weekend restrictions.
