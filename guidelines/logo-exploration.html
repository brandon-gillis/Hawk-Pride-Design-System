Why the current logo reads as AI
Too many ideas. Jeep, side-by-side, trees, hawk, mountains, dirt spray and three type styles, all at once. Nobody would draw it that way on purpose.
Rendered, not designed. Chrome gradients, distressed texture, glow and particles stand in for decisions.
Melted details. The grilles, wheels, roll cages and feathers look right from a distance and fall apart up close.
Generic type. Slanted "esports" lettering with no real spacing, kerning or family.
Doesn't survive a reduction test. At favicon, embroidery or sticker size it turns into mush, and it can't print in one color.
Rules for the rebuild
1. One idea: the hawk over the mountain.
2. Flat black and gold. No gradients, texture or glow.
3. Real, set type from one family, with deliberate spacing.
4. Plain geometry for any container.
5. Must read at 16px and in one color.
The brandmark itself still has AI traces (soft grey smudges inside the black, uneven stroke weight). These lockups use it as supplied. It should get a clean vector redraw before anything goes to print.
