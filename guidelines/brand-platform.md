# Brand platform (summary)
Condensed from the Hawk Pride brand context pack (Google Drive, 7 docs: 00-context … 06-open-decisions). Strategy governs brand meaning; the website pack governs product behavior.

## Locked
- **Promise:** A whole mountain of real off-road adventure, your way.
- **Tagline:** Go conquer something. (Hawk Pride does not define what conquering means. The customer does.)
- **Positioning (internal):** A mountain full of real off-road adventure, where everyone from recreational riders in mostly stock rigs to hardcore crawlers can find their kind of fun and have one hell of a weekend.
- **Mission:** Create one hell of an off-road weekend by making more of the mountain easier to discover, experience, and enjoy.
- **Emotional payoff:** A trip that feels genuinely worth taking, full of discovery, fun, shared experience and accomplishment, with enough left unexplored to want another one.
- **Principles:** Reveal what's already here · Keep the off-roading real · Take care of the basics · Let people make the weekend their own · Make the haul worth it · Leave something for next time.
- **Supporting lines:** "Ride what you came for. Find what you didn't." (discovery) · "Mild. Wild. And a whole lot in between." (range) · "A whole mountain of bad ideas." (merch/campaign only) · "Every trip should feel like an adventure worth remembering." (internal only)

## Hierarchy
1 Adventure → 2 Exploration & discovery → 3 The mountain & fun → 4 Challenge & accomplishment → 5 Something still waiting.

## Experience loop
Anticipate → Explore → Enjoy → Accomplish → Remember → Return.

## Audience
Center: regional recreational off-roaders (Jeep, 4x4, SxS, buggy) traveling with friends, family or a club for a trip-worthy weekend. Waiver data: TN 34%, AL 26%, MS 17%, GA 9%; median adult age 42–44; 200+ records list minors; many passengers, not just drivers; "Friends" is the top referral source. Often deciding Thursday/Friday on a phone.
Edges: families & mixed-skill groups (a capability, not the identity); hardcore crawlers (give the brand teeth, don't define it); event attendees (important, don't define ordinary weekends).

## Guardrails
- Reveal and organize the existing magic; don't overwrite it. No resort polish, no sanitizing, no over-programming.
- Don't compete on biggest, cheapest, most extreme, resort, or generic "something for everyone." Frame compactness as **density**.
- Don't promise unfinished capabilities (full trail-navigation system, curated adventure portfolio, consistent difficulty standards, completion rewards, Little Hawk) as if they exist today.

## Not yet established
Visual identity, color, typography, photography direction, graphic language. The tokens in this design system are a working proposal (black + gold from the badge, per the owner).
