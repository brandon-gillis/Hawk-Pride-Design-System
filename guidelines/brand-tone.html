Five traits

DirectLead with what people need: when, where, how much, what to bring.
PlayfulA grin, not a joke. Let the mountain be the fun part.
ConfidentWe know this mountain. No hedging, no hype.
InformalSay it like you would at the gate. Short words, no brochure talk.
MischievousComfortable with a little dirt and trouble. Never a dare.

Rule, not a trait: serious stays serious. Safety, closures, rules, waivers, weather and payments get none of the above.
